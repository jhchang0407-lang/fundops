## Vision

### 10x Check
FundOps becomes the operating system for any investment strategy. Strategy Marketplace where users share/download strategy profiles. Multi-strategy portfolios. Live broker integration. Natural language investment terminal. The strategy IS the product, the pipeline IS the moat.

### Platonic Ideal
Using FundOps feels like having a research analyst, portfolio manager, and risk officer sitting next to you. You see portfolio health at a glance, read a one-paragraph summary of why a swap makes sense, say "run the thesis on GOOGL" and get results in 3 minutes. The system never surprises you. Every number links to the data that produced it. After 6 months, your returns improve not because the AI is smarter, but because it's more disciplined.
