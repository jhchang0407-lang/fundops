# Active Memo Uses Research Memo Foundation

Superseded in part by ADR-0014: the Monitoring Plan is no longer treated as a visible final memo subsection; Memo now produces it as a separate structured output for Thesis Health.

The v2 Memo workflow starts clean and produces one user-facing Investment Memo artifact that replaces both the old Research Memo and the old lightweight Investment Memo. It does not produce separate Research Memo and Investment Memo artifacts. The new Investment Memo is built from the Research Memo foundation as a design lineage only: the rich company, filing, financial, valuation, table, sector-specific, and section-scoped evidence behavior from the old Research Memo should be preserved, while the old neutral report outline is replaced by a fixed strategy-neutral Investment Memo outline. There is no legacy memo data migration or backward-compatibility requirement for active v2 generation.

We are not extending the superseded lightweight four-section Investment Memo path as the foundation for the active workflow. Useful parts of that path may be reused, especially current-market setup language, online research prompts, valuation framing, and return-driver ideas, but its shorter structure and free-form writer flow should be deprecated for active Memo generation.

The new Investment Memo uses section-scoped evidence distribution. Memo-native filing and financial evidence, market intelligence evidence, valuation and peer evidence, strategy emphasis, and evidence-quality signals are gathered first, then distributed into section-specific evidence packages. Section writers should receive curated inputs for their section rather than the whole research corpus. Completed Thesis, IC Verdict, IC scores, IC rationale, and saved IC evidence are workflow provenance only; they should not become writer inputs or evidence for the Investment Memo argument.

The fixed final reading outline is Current Setup & Variant View, Business Quality, Industry and Growth, Financial Quality, Valuation, Risks, and Decision Summary & Monitoring Plan. The generation order may differ from this reading order: Business, Industry/Growth, Financial Quality, and Risks are core body sections; Risks should be generated before Valuation so downside drivers can inform assumptions; Current Setup is an opening synthesis generated late; Decision Summary is generated last.

The legacy Research Memo sections are folded into the new outline rather than kept as separate top-level sections. Company history becomes concise company context focused on recent developments and why the opportunity exists. Product/technology, moats, and management/capital allocation move into Business Quality. Customer analysis plus industry and competitive dynamics move into Industry and Growth. Financial Analysis and Peer Benchmarking merge into Financial Quality. Risk Assessment maps into Risks, Bear Case & Kill Criteria. The old Executive Summary and Conclusion behavior becomes Current Setup and Decision Summary, avoiding duplicate summaries.

Valuation uses a hybrid boundary. The model selection, valuation anchors, peer data, tables, sensitivity calculations, and final fair-value arithmetic should be deterministic or calculation-backed. The valuation writer may still make memo-native judgment calls about bull/base/bear assumptions, probability weights, key scenario drivers, and return drivers after reading relevant memo evidence. Deterministic math then grounds, validates, or corrects fair-value, upside, downside, and sensitivity outputs.

This decision preserves the expensive behavior that made the Research Memo valuable while making the output investment-oriented, comparable across opportunities, and less prone to hallucination from overly broad context. The main tradeoff is implementation complexity: we keep the richer distributor/research-agent/writer pattern instead of shipping the simpler four-section Investment Memo path, because Memo is the high-depth workflow and should justify its token spend.
