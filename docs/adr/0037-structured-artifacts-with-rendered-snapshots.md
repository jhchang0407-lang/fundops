# Structured Artifacts With Rendered Snapshots

FundOps will store readable completed workflow outputs as structured artifacts with schema versions, typed sections or fields, citations, evidence references, validation state, and render metadata, rather than storing rendered prose alone. Rendered markdown, text, HTML, or PDF-ready snapshots may be stored or generated for reading and export, but the Structured Workflow Artifact remains the canonical artifact body.

This applies across artifacts such as Screener snapshots, Completed Thesis, IC Verdicts, Investment Memos, Thesis Health records, Portfolio Review outputs, and Learning/Evals cards when they are readable or reopenable. Artifact Export should use the stored structured artifact or rendered snapshot and should not require re-running the workflow or reparsing prose to recover citations, watch items, valuation assumptions, or evidence links.
