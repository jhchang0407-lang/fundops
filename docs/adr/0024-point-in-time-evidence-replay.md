# Point-in-Time Evidence Supports Replay

FundOps will preserve point-in-time evidence for completed workflow outputs, decisions, memos, portfolio records, and Learning/Evals rather than allowing historical records to be silently reinterpreted through the latest available data. Canonical Evidence Records should distinguish the time the evidence describes, the time FundOps captured it, its source and quality, and any later supersession, restatement, correction, remapping, or revised finding.

Current projections may show the latest known state, but completed artifacts and historical decisions should remain replayable from the evidence available at the time. This avoids hindsight contamination in Learning/Evals and keeps old screens, theses, IC verdicts, memos, and portfolio history honest about what FundOps knew when they were created.
