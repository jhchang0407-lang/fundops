# Workflows Around a Shared Platform Spine

FundOps will be architected as workflow capabilities around shared retained records, canonical evidence, completed artifact identity, and current projections rather than as pages or agents that each own independent data stores. Screener, Thesis, IC Review, Memo, Thesis Health, Portfolio, Portfolio Review, and Learning/Evals should produce and consume the shared spine, while Dashboard, Company Page, Library, Archive Q&A, and workflow screens read projections tailored to their user-facing jobs.

This shape gives the platform room to grow without duplicating investment facts across functions. Orchestration coordinates runs and handoffs, but it does not own the evidence or artifacts produced by the workflow; projections make current screens fast and legible without becoming separate truth.
