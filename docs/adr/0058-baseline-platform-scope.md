# Baseline Platform Scope

The platform rebuild ships the full spine (identity, constitution versions and wiring, canonical evidence, periodized financials, durable runs, shared artifact identity, portfolio ledger, dashboard sources and responses, thesis health operational records, learning records, work queue, provenance, AI usage) and the primary product surfaces (FundOps Chat, Dashboard, Screener, Thesis, IC Review, Memo, Company Page, Library, Portfolio, Settings, Workflow Artifact Reader). Several institutional-layer capabilities are intentionally records-first at baseline: Risk & Exposure views, Institutional Review Packet exports, Audit Packages, the Agent-Native Workspace extension harness, broker import adapters, and FMP enrichment are represented by retained records and seams (decision register, approval records, evidence bundles, import-source fields) rather than full surfaces.

We chose this cut because the product principles require the control records to exist from day one — decisions, approvals, exceptions, provenance — while their dedicated review surfaces compose cleanly on top of retained records later. Building surfaces before the spine would have inverted that dependency.
