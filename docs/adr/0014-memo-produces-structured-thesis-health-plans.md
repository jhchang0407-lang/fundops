# Memo Produces Structured Thesis Health Plans

Memo owns creation of the Investment Memo Monitoring Plan, but that plan is persisted as separate structured records for Memo-Backed Thesis Health rather than a visible subsection inside the rendered Investment Memo. The visible memo ends with Decision Summary; Thesis Health reads memo-versioned structured watch items directly, validates them against the Supported Thesis Health Field Catalog, and refreshes them through metadata-gated filing checks rather than reparsing memo prose.

This supersedes the part of ADR-0013 that treated "Decision Summary & Monitoring Plan" as one visible final memo section. We chose the split because the monitoring plan must be durable, versioned, validated, queue-aware, and usable for long-horizon learning across held and non-held memo-backed tickers, while the memo itself should remain a readable investment artifact.

Thesis Health operational records are the source of truth for plans, watch items, refresh results, and data gaps. They should distinguish the monitoring plan, watch items, refresh runs, and per-item checks while retaining raw structured plan JSON for audit/export. Quantitative watch items must name a measurement cadence and lookback basis allowed by the Supported Thesis Health Field Catalog so confirmation periods and compared evidence windows are computable and unambiguous, and status evaluation after plan creation is deterministic rather than LLM-rejudged. Active watch items keep their current state derived from the latest accepted per-item check so current UI reads are simple while history remains append-only. Timeline or Dashboard events may mirror material breaks or degraded monitoring, but they do not own routine watch-item state.

Thesis Health requires retained quarterly financial history, not just annual snapshots. The starting retention target is twelve quarters, or roughly three years, plus five fiscal years of annual fundamentals when available. Derived Q4 flow metrics may be used when they can be proven from full-year 10-K values minus the first three quarters, but the derivation provenance must be retained and incomplete derivations become data gaps.

When active watch items are created, Thesis Health records memo-time baseline checks so fresh memo-backed tracking starts with grounded current state instead of waiting for a scheduled filing refresh. A memo is thesis-health ready only if at least one watch item has a catalog-allowed metric, measurement cadence, lookback basis, and available baseline evidence. Baseline checks should usually initialize items as intact, watch, unknown, or data gap; broken status should generally come from post-memo refresh evidence unless the monitoring plan explicitly defines an immediate kill criterion.

When a new Completed Memo becomes active, prior plans and watch items are frozen as historical rather than automatically carried forward into the new active plan. Similar watch items across memo versions may be linked later for continuity analysis, but each active plan should represent the active memo's own judgment.

Unsupported watch items may be retained internally for future monitoring sources, but they are hidden from the MVP Thesis Health UI and do not affect status. Thesis Health updates are scheduled or Dashboard-initiated, use shared provider queue limits, prioritize held tickers over non-held tickers, and only run full quantitative recalculation when a new relevant 10-Q or 10-K is detected.
